# Outline

Main idea: Designers can ritualize time, focusing on depth rather than productive clock-time (Han). Relying on foreground/background-shifting (Eno) as well as repetition, returning, and ceremonious intervals (Vesper5/Ritual of the Moon).

## Ritualization

Use Han's _Scent of Time_ and _Disappearance of Rituals_ to lay the foundation for the ideas to be explored. My original proposal leaned more into the idea of "ambient media" as the precedent, which is interesting. ("We will explore how adjusting, slowing, stopping, and ignoring time can effect the potentials for playful and contemplative experiences.") Can we build on ambient media and talk about how it encourages a ritualization? An outside-of-time-ness? These modes of time could prove interesting avenues of inquiry as well. One precedent each for slowing, stopping, ignoring, etc? The proposal also leaned into ambient games, but I think maybe this is the wrong direction? Leans too far towards "idle" games? If so, does this move away from "Ritualization" as a sole technique, instead focusing on "new ways to engage with time"...? Perhaps too broad? Also we might move too far into the "enstrangement of time" area.
